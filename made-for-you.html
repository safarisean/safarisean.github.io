Made for You

Grown-ups don’t get enough recess.

When we were kids, somebody rang a bell and sent us outside to play. Now the only thing that rings is the dryer.

I think grown-ups have a play deficit. I don’t mean we need another hobby to get good at. I mean we could use more time around a table, laughing with people we like, doing something that doesn’t need to be useful.

So I make games out of the stuff only your people would get: the dog with four nicknames, the creek behind Grandma’s house, the gas station everybody insists has the best pizza.

Because sometimes the best way to get grown-ups to play is to put that gas station on a card and make them defend it.
