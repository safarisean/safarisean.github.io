The Game

What is The Pecking Order?

A game about ranking things that probably shouldn't be ranked.

Five cards are drawn from the deck. One player is chosen, and everyone else secretly ranks those five cards from best to worst based on how they think that player would rank them.

Then the chosen player reveals their actual ranking.

Compare your answers and find out who knows them best.

You might know your best friend's birthday, favorite color, and middle name. But do you know where they'd rank gas station pizza?
